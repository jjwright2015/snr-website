Race 16 — Richmond Raceway

Banks Does it Again — Charges from 16th to Win at Richmond Raceway

June 20, 2026 · SNR League · 2 min read

Joshua Banks continued his late-season surge on Saturday Night at Richmond Raceway, charging from 16th to lead 48 laps and claim his third victory of the Season 6 campaign. It was a dramatic night at the three-quarter mile oval — one that could have major implications heading into the final regular season race at Chicagoland.

Jeff J. Wright started from the pole and was nothing short of dominant early, leading a race-high 128 laps and looking every bit like a man destined for his 6th win of the season. That all changed on Lap 83 when Wright suffered a wheel force feedback malfunction that disrupted his feel for the car, gradually shuffling him toward the back of the field and ultimately ending any chance of a victory.

A.J. Stravato took command of the race lead on Lap 106 for 20 laps — but an untimely caution fell just as Stravato pitted from the lead, leaving him exposed in traffic. Stravato then got collected in a wreck with J R Shepherd that effectively ended both drivers’ hopes of winning, with Stravato ultimately finishing 27th — a devastating result in the championship battle.

Joshua Banks, armed with a savvy two-tire strategy call by his team, vaulted to the race lead and never looked back. When the checkered flag flew, Banks crossed the line ahead of Kenny Kibbey by 2.155 seconds, with Elliott Butler delivering his best finish of the season in 3rd. Ryan Carwile charged to 4th and Angelo Hronis rounded out the top 5. Bryce Shoemaker crossed the line in 6th, tying his best finish of the season that came earlier in the year at Nashville Superspeedway. Seth Hatchel finished 7th, Austin Boelke 8th, Caleb Weekley 9th, and Matt Shinoski rounded out the top 10.

The championship standings remain razor-tight heading into the regular season finale at Chicagoland. A.J. Stravato clings to the points lead at 595 despite his tough night, just 23 ahead of Bailey Turner (572) — who did not race at Richmond — and Kenny Kibbey (571) now breathing down both their necks just 1 point behind Turner. The top 14 drivers will lock themselves into the Chase when the regular season concludes, and with just one race remaining before the playoffs begin, every point matters.

The SNR Cup Series heads to Chicagoland Speedway next Saturday Night for the regular season finale. Tune into VSPEED for full live coverage!
