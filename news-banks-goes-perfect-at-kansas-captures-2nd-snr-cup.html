Chase Race 5 — Season Finale — Kansas Speedway

Banks Goes Perfect at Kansas — Captures 2nd SNR Cup Series Championship

August 8, 2026 · SNR League · 2 min read

Joshua Banks turned in the drive of his career when it mattered most. The two-time SNR Cup Series champion won the pole, led a race-high 62 laps, and won the Season 6 finale at Kansas Speedway — but the road to Victory Lane and the championship was anything but smooth.

Early in the race, contact with Jeff J. Wright for the lead on lap 15 sent Banks sliding through the infield in a moment that looked like it may have ended his championship hopes on the spot. Banks managed to save the car, but the damage was done — he fell all the way back to 14th place. What followed was one of the most impressive drives in league history, as Banks methodically worked his way back through the field, retook the race lead, and never looked back the rest of the way.

The championship battle was just as dramatic on points. Bailey Turner entered the night holding a 10-point lead over Banks, but a decision to stay out on older tires during a lap 8 caution shuffled Turner to the back of the field. Turner mounted an impressive charge of his own, clawing his way back to a 4th-place finish — but it wasn’t enough. Banks’ win vaulted him past Turner by 5 points at the checkered flag, capturing the Season 6 championship.

It’s the 5th win of the season for Banks and his 2nd career victory at Kansas Speedway. Dakota Floyd2 was the only other driver to lead significant laps, pacing the field for 35 laps and coming home 2nd. Kenny Kibbey rounded out the podium in 3rd, with Turner in 4th and A.J. Stravato in 5th. J R Shepherd, Angelo Hronis, Jeff J. Wright, Justin Richie, and Reggie Ortega completed the top 10. Only a single caution flew on the night, for 3 laps, as seven different drivers traded the lead over nine lead changes.

Final Season 6 Championship Standings

Joshua Banks closes out Season 6 as champion with 2,240 points, edging Bailey Turner by just 5 points at 2,235 in one of the tightest title fights in league history.
