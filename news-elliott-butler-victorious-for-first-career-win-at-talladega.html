Chase Race 1 — Talladega Superspeedway

Elliott Butler Victorious for First Career Win at Talladega in the Project Grace 250!

July 11, 2026 · SNR League · 3 min read

Elliott Butler made history Saturday Night at Talladega Superspeedway, charging from 19th to lead a race-high 30 laps and claim his first ever career SNR Cup Series victory in the Project Grace 250 — Chase Race 1 of 5. Butler becomes the 28th different driver to win a race in the SNR Cup Series — and he couldn’t have picked a bigger stage to do it.

Talladega delivered exactly what it always promises — chaos, drama, and heartbreak in equal measure. With 14 leaders and 18 lead changes across 94 laps and just one caution on the night, the Monster delivered a race that will be remembered for a long time. Butler, starting 19th, methodically worked his way through the field and found himself out front at the right moments, ultimately holding off Joshua Banks by 0.264 seconds at the checkered flag. Bailey Turner crossed the line in 3rd, Chad Clinger 4th, and Rhea Massey 5th. Bryce Shoemaker had a strong run to 6th, followed by Jeff J. Wright in 7th — who led 10 laps and showed strong speed throughout the night.

The race’s defining moment came on Lap 21 when the Talladega “Big One” struck. Points leader Kenny Kibbey and Caleb Weekley were swept up in the wreck, ending their hopes for a top finish and costing them dearly in the standings. Kibbey’s 25-point bonus for winning the regular season championship helped cushion the blow — but he still fell from 1st to 4th in the Chase standings. Weekley was hit even harder, sliding from 14th to 12th.
