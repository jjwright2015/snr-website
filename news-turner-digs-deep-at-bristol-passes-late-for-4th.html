Chase Race 4 — Bristol Motor Speedway

Turner Digs Deep at Bristol — Passes Late for 4th Win of the Season

August 1, 2026 · SNR League · 2 min read

Bailey Turner is putting together a championship-caliber close to the season. The points leader added another trophy to his case Saturday Night at Bristol Motor Speedway, picking off Bryce Shoemaker for the race lead on lap 184 and holding off a hard-charging Jeff J. Wright the rest of the way to capture his 4th win of the season — and his 2nd career SNR Cup Series win at The Last Great Coliseum.

The race was shaping up to be a clean one early, running caution-free through the first 60 laps. But that calm didn’t last — 11 cautions flew over the remaining 140 laps, shuffling strategy and shaking up the running order all night long. Six different drivers led the race across 9 lead changes. Dakota Floyd2 started on the pole and led a race-high 77 laps before an early exit ended his night, while Jeff J. Wright spent long stretches out front with 47 laps led of his own. Joshua Banks (21 laps led), Bryce Shoemaker (17), Ryan Carwile (11), and eventually Turner (27) all took their turns at the point as the laps wound down.

It was Turner who made the winning move when it mattered most, working his way past Shoemaker for the lead with 16 laps to go and running clean the rest of the way — finishing the night with zero incidents. Wright charged hard in the closing laps but couldn’t quite complete the pass, settling for a strong runner-up finish. Joshua Banks came home 3rd, followed by Phillip Brewer in 4th and J R Shepherd in 5th. Angelo Hronis, Reggie Ortega, Justin Richie, Ben Laughter, and Casey Lakatos rounded out the top 10.

Chase Standings — 1 Race Remaining

Turner’s win extended his championship lead to 2,195 points, now 10 points clear of Joshua Banks in 2nd at 2,185.
