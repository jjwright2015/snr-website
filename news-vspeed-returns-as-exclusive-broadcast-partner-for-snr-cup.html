VSPEED Returns as Exclusive Broadcast Partner for SNR Cup Series Season 7

November 17, 2026 · SNR League · 1 min read

The Saturday Night Racing League is proud to announce that it has reached an agreement with VSPEED to broadcast the SNR Cup Series in Season 7. VSPEED will continue as the league’s exclusive broadcast partner, bringing every race live to fans on Saturday nights.

VSPEED has been with the Saturday Night Racing League since the very beginning. When the league was established in 2023, VSPEED was there to bring the action to fans — and it has been the home of SNR racing ever since. From the first green flag of Season 1 to the championship finale of Season 6, VSPEED has been a big part of what makes Saturday night special.

We are extremely excited to have VSPEED return for Season 7, and we’re looking forward to a fantastic season of racing ahead. Thank you to VSPEED for your continued partnership, and to every driver and fan who tunes in each week.

The SNR Cup Series Season 7 schedule will be announced in the coming weeks — stay tuned, and make sure you’re subscribed to VSPEED so you never miss a race.
