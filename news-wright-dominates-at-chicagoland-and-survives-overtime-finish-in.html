Race 17 — Chicagoland Speedway

Wright Dominates at Chicagoland and Survives Overtime Finish in Regular Season Finale

June 27, 2026 · SNR League · 2 min read

Jeff J. Wright closed out the regular season in style Saturday Night at Chicagoland Speedway, starting from the pole, leading a dominant 118 of 143 laps, and surviving a chaotic overtime finish to claim his 6th victory of the Season 6 campaign — the most wins of any driver in the series this season.

Wright was in a class of his own for the majority of the night, but it was Phillip Brewer who made things interesting. Starting from 3rd, Brewer was relentless in pressuring Wright throughout the race, trading the lead back and forth on multiple occasions and refusing to let the #48 pull away cleanly. Wright ultimately had the pace to reclaim the lead each time and hold on when it counted. Adam Cabot also made his presence felt, charging from 17th to lead 7 laps, with Joshua Banks adding 4, Christopher Hodges 4, Bailey Turner 2, Brandon Wallace 3, Brian Chambliss 3, and Dakota Floyd2 rounding out the lap leaders with 1.

Three cautions for 7 laps — including multiple incidents in the closing stages — triggered a Green-White-Checkered overtime finish that pushed the race past its scheduled distance. Dakota Floyd2 received a 10-point penalty for causing a caution in the final 15% of the race — a costly miscue that dropped him one position in the final regular season standings, relegating him to 2,002 points to begin the Chase instead of the 2,003 that Justin Richie will now start with. Ben Laughter also received a 10-point penalty for the same offense. Through all the chaos, Wright kept his composure and took the checkered flag. Phillip Brewer crossed the line in 2nd, Adam Cabot charged to 3rd, Elliott Butler finished 4th, and Kenny Kibbey rounded out the top 5. Seth Hatchel finished 6th, Joshua Banks 7th, Casey Lakatos 8th, Reggie Ortega 9th, and Angelo Hronis rounded out the top 10.
