Race 15 — Sonoma Raceway

Wright Flawless at Sonoma — Leads Every Lap for 5th Win of the Season

June 13, 2026 · SNR League · 2 min read

Jeff J. Wright delivered one of the most dominant performances of the Season 6 campaign on Saturday Night at Sonoma Raceway, leading every single lap of the 55-lap race to claim his 5th victory of the season and cement his status as the most dangerous driver at Sonoma Raceway in the Saturday Night Racing League Cup Series.

Starting from the pole, Wright got away cleanly at the green flag — but it was A.J. Stravato who briefly took the lead on the opening lap before overshooting Turn 11 late in Lap 1, giving Wright the opening he needed. Wright made the pass, recaptured the lead and never looked back, running unchallenged for the remaining 54 laps to win by a commanding 10.360-second margin over Stravato.

With zero cautions, zero lead changes, and just one leader across 55 laps, it was as close to a perfect race as you can run. Wright also set the fastest lap of the race on Lap 31 with a 1:17.690 — underscoring just how complete his performance was on the day.

It marks Wright’s 2nd career series victory at Sonoma Raceway, having also won at the track back in Season 1. He now remains undefeated at Sonoma in the SNR Cup Series.

Stravato crossed the line in 2nd after the early miscue, with J R Shepherd rounding out the podium in 3rd. Bailey Turner came home 4th and Dakota Floyd2 charged from 7th to finish 5th.

In the championship picture, A.J. Stravato holds the lead with 578 points — just 6 ahead of Bailey Turner (572) as the two remain separated by less than a single race win in what has become the tightest title fight in recent series memory. Kenny Kibbey sits 3rd at 529 points with just 2 races remaining in the regular season before the Chase begins.

The SNR Cup Series heads to Richmond Raceway next Saturday Night for Race 16 — the penultimate regular season event. Tune into VSPEED for full live coverage!
