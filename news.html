Race 14 — Atlanta Motor Speedway June 6, 2026

Banks Battles from 13th to Victory Lane at Atlanta Motor Speedway

The Saturday Night Racing League Cup Series returned to the old school legacy 2008 version of Atlanta Motor Speedway on Saturday Night for Race 14 of 22, and it was Joshua Banks delivering a statement performance — charging from 13th to lead a race-high 68 laps and claim his second victory of the Season 6 campaign.

Kenny Kibbey set the tone early by earning the pole, but it was outside polesitter Adam Cabot who got the jump at the start, leading the first 26 laps of the race before J R Shepherd took command on the long run. The race quickly developed into a strategic battle between two differing pit strategies. Cabot, Jeff J. Wright, and Bailey Turner led the way on the 3-stop strategy, while Shepherd ran in control on the 2-stop strategy — before ultimately pivoting to the 3-stop strategy as well, a decision that may have cost him a shot at Victory Lane. The 3-stop runners cycled through their pit stops around Lap 87, and it was ultimately Joshua Banks and his dominating long run pace that made the 2-stop strategy work to perfection, taking command and building the margin he needed to hold on for the victory.

Banks crossed the line ahead of A.J. Stravato in 2nd and Seth Hatchel in 3rd. Adam Cabot finished a solid 4th with Rhea Massey rounding out the top 5. Championship contenders Bailey Turner and Kenny Kibbey came home 10th and 12th respectively. Jeff J. Wright, who appeared to be in contention for a top 5 finish, once again suffered a disconnection due to a hardware issue that cost him valuable positions and ultimately relegated him to 29th at the finish.

The result tightens the championship battle at the top — A.J. Stravato reclaims the points lead with 536, just 4 ahead of Bailey Turner (532) as the two continue their season-long duel with just 3 races remaining in the regular season (Sonoma, Richmond, and Chicagoland). Kenny Kibbey holds 3rd at 497, a full 39 points back.

Race 13 — Nashville Superspeedway May 30, 2026

Bailey Turner Claims Third Win of Season 6 at Nashville Superspeedway

The Saturday Night Racing League Cup Series returned to Nashville Superspeedway for the third time in series history on Saturday Night for Race 13 of 22, and it was Bailey Turner taking the checkered flag in the #24 Celsius/Axalta Chevrolet to claim his third victory of the Season 6 campaign — and with it, the championship points lead.

J R Shepherd set the pace in qualifying to earn the pole, but it was Turner and Adam Cabot who controlled the majority of the action. Cabot led 58 laps and Turner 64 in a spirited battle at the front, with Rhea Massey also making his presence felt by leading 14 laps. Casey Lakatos added 8, Phillip Brewer 3, and pole-sitter Shepherd led 1 — giving the race 8 leaders and 10 lead changes across 150 green flag laps with not a single caution on the night.

When it mattered most, Turner had the car to beat. He took the lead for good and pulled away to claim the win, with Cabot finishing a strong 2nd for his second consecutive podium. Dakota Floyd2 charged from 14th to complete the podium in 3rd, while Phillip Brewer finished 4th and Kenny Kibbey rounded out the top 5.

Championship leader A.J. Stravato came home 10th, and the result has major implications at the top of the standings. Turner’s win vaults him into the points lead with 498 points — just 4 ahead of Stravato (494) in what has become a two-driver title fight with 9 races remaining in the regular season. Kenny Kibbey sits 3rd at 465, a full 33 points behind Turner.

The SNR Cup Series is back in action next Saturday Night at the Old School Legacy 2008 version of Atlanta Motor Speedway. Tune into VSPEED for full live coverage!

Race 12 — Charlotte Motor Speedway May 23, 2026

Wright Dominates the TeamBoss 300 at Charlotte Motor Speedway

Jeff J. Wright put on a masterclass performance Saturday Night at Charlotte Motor Speedway, leading a dominant 150 of 200 laps to claim victory in the TeamBoss 300 — his 4th win of the Season 6 campaign for the Saturday Night Racing League Cup Series.

Starting from 3rd, Wright wasted no time working his way to the front and was never truly challenged once he took command. Phillip Brewer, who started from the pole, led the opening 20 laps before Wright seized control. Seth Hatchel led 12 laps, while Adam Cabot led 8 — including Lap 18, where the Saturday Night Racing League and VSPEED paused to pay tribute to NASCAR legend Kyle Busch — #8 — with a special “Forever Rowdy” segment in honor of the two-time Cup Series champion who tragically passed away earlier in the week on Thursday.

The lone caution of the night flew on Lap 136 for an incident between Chad Clinger and Joshua Banks. The field returned to green on Lap 140, setting up a dramatic 60-lap fuel mileage run to the checkered flag. Wright and Adam Cabot waged a battle to the end, but Wright had enough in the tank to hold off Cabot and take the victory.

When the checkered flag flew, it was Wright in Victory Lane, followed by Cabot in 2nd — his best finish of the season — and Ryan Carwile charging from 15th to complete the podium in 3rd. Bailey Turner finished 4th while championship leader A.J. Stravato came home 7th after starting 18th.

Despite his dominant victory and 4th win of the season, Wright sits just 7th in the championship standings — a deficit largely due to DNFs at EchoPark Speedway and Dover Motor Speedway caused by internet and power related issues that cost him valuable points. A.J. Stravato leads the championship with 460 points, 17 ahead of Bailey Turner (443) and 34 ahead of Kenny Kibbey (426) as the top three remain tightly bunched heading into the second half of the season.

Race 11 — Dover Motor Speedway May 16, 2026

Stravato Makes it Three — Wins at Dover Motor Speedway to Extend Championship Lead

The Saturday Night Racing League Cup Series rolled into Dover Motor Speedway on Saturday Night for Race 11 of 22, and once again it was A.J. Stravato standing in Victory Lane — claiming his third win of the Season 6 campaign and firmly planting his flag atop the championship standings.

In a 180-lap race at The Monster Mile, J R Shepherd set the early pace from the front row, leading a race-high 71 laps in the opening stages of the event. Seth Hatchel, who started from the pole, took over the lead through the middle portions of the race, pacing the field for 65 laps. Brandon Wallace also had a strong showing, leading 6 laps, with Phillip Brewer adding 3 more to his name.

Despite not leading the most laps, Stravato — starting 4th — had the fastest car when it mattered most, taking command for the final 35 laps and never looking back. Polesitter Seth Hatchel crossed the line in 2nd, while Adam Cabot charged from 16th to complete the podium with an impressive 3rd place finish.

Two cautions flew over the 180 laps. The first was triggered by a Chad Clinger incident that collected Ryan Carwile, who suffered an early exit and was scored 33rd. The second yellow flew after Casey Lakatos suffered an internet-related issue that ultimately collected Chad Clinger, ending his night with a 27th place finish.

The win extends Stravato’s championship lead to 423 points — 20 ahead of Bailey Turner (403) and 24 ahead of Kenny Kibbey (399) as the three-way title fight at the top continues to tighten heading into the back half of the season.

Race 10 — Texas Motor Speedway May 2, 2026

A.J. Stravato Dominates Texas Motor Speedway for Second Win of Season 6

The Saturday Night Racing League Cup Series made its return to Texas Motor Speedway on Saturday Night for Race 10 of 22, and it was A.J. Stravato putting on a dominant performance from start to finish.

Stravato, starting from the 5th position, wasted little time working his way to the front and ultimately led a commanding 109 of 140 laps en route to his second victory of the season. The win moves Stravato from 3rd to 1st in the championship standings with 368 points, nudging out Bailey Turner (364) and Kenny Kibbey (362) in what is shaping up to be an incredibly tight title battle.

J R Shepherd set fast time in qualifying to claim the pole position but would ultimately finish 5th after leading 12 laps himself. Joshua Banks charged from 18th to finish 2nd, while Phillip Brewer rounded out the podium in 3rd — his best finish of the season.

Three cautions slowed the action over 140 laps with 7 different leaders and 11 lead changes keeping the field on edge throughout the night. Penalties were handed down post-race to Mikal Jones, Reggie Ortega, and Caleb Weekley — each docked 5 points for causing cautions.

The Saturday Night Racing League Cup Series is off for Mother’s Day Weekend and will return in two weeks at Dover Motor Speedway on Saturday, May 16th at 10:00 PM EDT. Tune into VSPEED for full live coverage!

Race 9 — EchoPark Speedway April 25, 2026

Telgenhof Storms from P32 to Victory Lane at EchoPark Speedway

The Saturday Night Racing League Cup Series visited newly paved EchoPark Speedway for the first time in series history last Saturday Night and the on-track action did not disappoint!

Only one yellow flag flew on lap 47 of the 130 laps for an accident involving front-row starter #1 Eldon Stewart, who would DNF for a 38th place finish in the 40-car field.

Clean and green the rest of the night, but the battle for the lead never subsided. The race saw 7 different race leaders through the course of the night. #48 Jeff Wright led a race-high 82 laps before a hardware failure ultimately took him out of contention, settling for 34th. #35 Joshua Banks led 17 laps, followed by #98 JR Shepherd (14) and #24 Bailey Turner (11) as the only double-digit lap leaders of the night.

After green flag pit stops cycled out on lap 117, it was Bailey Turner taking the race lead followed by Joshua Banks, Andrew Telgenhof, Chad Clinger, and Phillip Brewer. Turner would continue to lead until Telgenhof, Clinger, and Brewer formed an outside lane to complete a pass with 3 laps to go.

With help from Chad Clinger, it was Andrew Telgenhof taking the checkered flag for his 2nd victory of the season. Telgenhof won the SNR 500 at Daytona International Speedway on February 21st, 2026 and is now 1 of 3 drivers with multiple victories in Season 6 (Wright 3x, Turner 2x).

Telgenhof’s victory at EchoPark Speedway surged him +9 positions in the standings, where he now sits tied for 15th with Max Kost — just 9 points back of the final Chase transfer spot, currently held by Chuck Sweeting in 14th.

The SNR Cup Series heads to Texas Motor Speedway next Saturday, May 2nd, on the newly repaved configuration. The last time the Cup Series visited Texas was Season 4 on February 8th, 2025, where Bailey Turner scored the victory.

Tune into VSPEED next Saturday Night at 10:00 PM EST for all the on-track action!
